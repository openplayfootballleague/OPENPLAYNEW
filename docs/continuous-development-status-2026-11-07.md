New leagues are unpublished and new seasons are draft/private by default; no data was seeded.\n- **League and season lifecycle controls implemented:** administrators can publish/unpublish leagues and move seasons through draft → registration open → active → completed → archived. The admin dashboard includes scheduling, result entry, status controls and a fixture register.\n- **Result publication hardened:** both teams must have submitted and locked team sheets; every goal must be assigned to a player on the scoring team's sheet, repeated scorers are allowed, and the score must match the scorer count. The database stores draft and submitted timestamps separately; submitted sheets cannot be overwritten by the captain.
